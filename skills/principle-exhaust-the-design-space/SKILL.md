# Exhaust the Design Space

For novel UI interactions, open architectural choices, or product decisions where experience depends on feel, build 2-3 structurally distinct prototypes or sketches and compare them side by side before committing to a design. Variations of one shape do not count.

Skip this exploration for established implementation patterns, bug fixes or refactors with a clear target state, and constraints that leave only one viable approach.
